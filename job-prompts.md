# Job prompts: thinking through the Associated Bank role

Paste one of these into a new session. Do them in order, or pick the part you need.
The job is Principal Applied AI Product Manager at Associated Bank (I'm interviewing for it).

## Part 1: Finding problems
> I'm interviewing for the Principal Applied AI Product Manager role at Associated Bank (job notes are in NOW.md). Help me think through how I'd find the right problems to solve for commercial bankers. Cover who I'd talk to first, what I'd ask them, how I'd spot where they lose time or miss sales, and how I'd decide which problems AI can actually help with. Ask me one question at a time, and tell me when you're guessing about how the bank works.

## Part 2: Shaping the product
> I'm interviewing for the Principal Applied AI Product Manager role at Associated Bank (job notes are in NOW.md). Help me think through how I'd turn a banker's problem into a clear product plan: the outcome, what to build first, user stories (short descriptions of what a user needs), and how I'd measure success. Use one realistic example problem to walk through it. Ask me one question at a time.

## Part 3: Getting it approved
> I'm interviewing for the Principal Applied AI Product Manager role at Associated Bank (job notes are in NOW.md). Help me think through how I'd get an AI tool through a bank's risk, compliance, privacy and responsible-AI reviews without stalling, building those checks in from the start. Explain the bank terms in plain English. Tell me clearly what you know for sure and what I should verify. Ask me one question at a time.

## Part 4: Getting it used
> I'm interviewing for the Principal Applied AI Product Manager role at Associated Bank (job notes are in NOW.md). Help me think through how I'd get busy bankers to actually adopt a new AI tool, and how I'd use usage numbers and feedback to improve it over time. Ask me one question at a time.
