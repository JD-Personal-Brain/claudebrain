The idea

Three kinds of work, plus partner sign-offs

Each project moves through the same six steps. Every task in every step is marked by who does it. Each project leaves the machine more automated than it found it.

Auto

Runs on its own

A workflow or agent does it and tells the right person. No one has to remember it.

Draft

AI drafts, a person checks

AI does the first version, and a named person approves it.

Me

Only a person

Decisions, agreement between people, relationships and trade-offs. This is where the product manager's hours go.

Partner

Partner sign-off

A decision only a named partner can make, such as risk or compliance. Never automated.

At every gate

The automation check

Before each go/no-go, ask three questions of every task done by a person or drafted by AI:

  1. Did it repeat?Will it come up again, in this project or the next one?
  2. Is it stable?Did we do it the same way at least twice?
  3. Is it safe alone?It makes no decision, nothing reaches a client, and bank rules allow it.

Three yeses: move the task one kind toward Auto before the next project. If an automated task goes wrong, a person takes it back until it's fixed.
