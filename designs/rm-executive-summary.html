The problem

Relationship managers (RMs) win the bank's commercial business, but at many commercial banks they spend only about 25–30% of their time with clients (McKinsey). The rest goes to meeting prep, call notes, CRM updates and hunting for the next opportunity. Better insight only pays off if RMs have the time to act on it.

The product: The RM Assistant

Each RM gets their own AI assistant, inside the tools they already use: Salesforce, Teams and Outlook. It helps the RM win business and frees up the time to win it. It drafts and suggests; the RM always decides. It never contacts a client on its own, never sets a price and never makes a credit decision. What it is and all its capabilities.

Later waves add early warning on clients who may leave, referral prompts and prospect lookalikes, then client Q&A and AI sales coaching.

The value

  • More revenue per RM: pipeline and wins from the assistant's suggestions. McKinsey reports about 3–15% higher revenue per RM where banks rework a frontline area end to end (an outside benchmark, not our target).
  • More time with clients: hours saved each week, reported as hours, not turned into revenue.
  • Proven, not assumed: pilot RMs are compared with similar RMs not yet using it, and Finance agrees the method first. Targets are set from the bank's own numbers.
