Map 1 · every product

How we deliver a product

The six steps of the Product Workflow, each ending in a gate, with its 44 sub-steps underneath and the governance thread (the risk work) under each gate. After the first pass, steps 3 to 6 repeat in short cycles.

  1. Step 1DiscoverWhich problem or opportunity is worth solving first?
    Gate: Sponsor agrees on the target, the measure and today's baseline
    Governance Risk partner briefed on the idea
  2. Step 2DefineWhat exactly do we build first, and how will we know it worked?
    Gate: Brief, data contract and risk rating agreed; value model shows payback within [X] months
    Governance Risk intake and early rating; data use approved
  3. Step 3BuildWhat's the smallest version users will use and trust?
    Gate: Meets the quality bar in testing; every control is built and passes its control test
    Governance Controls built in; test set created
  4. Step 4Govern and testIs it safe, fair and controlled?
    Gate: All sign-offs recorded; monitoring plan agreed
    Governance Formal validation and sign-offs
  5. Step 5Release and adoptWill users use it every week?
    Gate: Weekly use and leading results hit targets; no serious incidents; long-term owner confirmed before scaling
    Governance Live monitoring; incident process
  6. Step 6Measure and iterateIs it delivering the outcome, and what comes next?
    Gate: Each month (and at the end of each feature wave): keep, change or stop each AI product; each quarter: invest more, adjust or stop overall
    Governance Ongoing monitoring

Map 2 · The RM Assistant

How an RM uses the product

An RM's week, with each of the product's workflows placed where it helps. Every RM-facing workflow (B1 to B8) has a point where the RM decides, and nothing reaches a client without the RM. B9 is the release routine behind them.

⚠️ Grouping the workflows into these four stages is my framing of the plan. Confirm it with RMs in Discover.
  1. Stage 1Find the opportunity or riskWhich clients should I call, and why?
  2. Stage 2Prepare (or any time)What do I need to know before I talk to them? Client Q&A (B7) works at any stage.
  3. Stage 3Meet and follow upWhat did we agree, and what happens next?
  4. Stage 4Log itIs the client record up to date?

B1 Monday opportunity list

Starts when
Every night (signals); every Monday morning (the Monday send).
Agents
A1, A2, M30
Step by step
  1. A1 scans clients and writes signals.
  2. A2 writes an insight for each.
  3. Insights appear in the assistant's panel on the Salesforce client record (no record field changes).
  4. Every Monday, a scheduled send (Power Automate) messages each RM their top [X] insights in Teams or Outlook, ranked by the agreed score, with links to each client record; it messages only the RM (and, if agreed, their manager), and sends a short "nothing new" note when there's nothing.
  5. Decision point The RM accepts, rejects (with a reason) or changes each insight.
  6. On accept, the RM creates an opportunity in Salesforce, tagged "AI-sourced."
  7. M30 sorts the feedback.
  8. Managers review the list in the weekly pipeline meeting, or whatever team meeting they already run (⚠️ to confirm).
Logged
Signals, insights, Monday sends, opens, decisions and reasons, opportunities created.
Measured by
Share of insights acted on; pipeline from AI-sourced opportunities.
