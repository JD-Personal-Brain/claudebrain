EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The approach in one page

CORE ASSUMPTIONMuch of what RMs need is standard across banks: client briefs, meeting follow-ups, product-gap opportunities, early warnings and referral prompts. These can be deployed quickly on platforms the bank already uses. They form the base; everything else is iterated and grown on top.
01Deploy the standard base fast, aimed at revenueBuild a working prototype in week 1, shape it with RMs, and get the first features live within weeks.
02Pair insights with timeBetter opportunities only pay off if RMs have time to act on them, so the base includes time savers (briefs, meeting follow-ups, call notes) alongside revenue insights.
03Govern from day one, and prove itRisk partners are involved from the first week. Results are measured against RMs not yet using the tool, so revenue is never credited to AI by mistake.
The plan (estimate)A working prototype in week 1, built alongside discovery. The meeting follow-up goes to pilot RMs in about weeks 2–4, and call notes into Salesforce and the insight features in about weeks 4–6, then small releases every week or two and a new wave about every five weeks. The pace is set by data access and AI review, not by build time. A first read on closed revenue comes at about month 6.
What I'd needA business sponsor, time with 6–10 RMs and their managers, named data owners, an early risk partner, and a willing pilot group.

1 · THE OUTCOME

Two goals that work together

More revenue opportunitiesBetter insight into which clients to call, and why.
More time to act on themLess time on meeting prep, call notes and CRM updates. RMs at many commercial banks spend only about 25–30% of their time with clients, according to McKinsey.

New revenue can come from three levers. Finding out which is biggest is the first job.

01Win new clientsProspects that look like our best clients, and referrals from other parts of the bank.
02Grow existing clientsClients who need a product they don't have yet, such as treasury management, including referrals between teams (RM to treasury specialist).
03Keep clientsEarly signs a client may leave, such as falling balances or less activity.

5 · HOW WE DEPLOY AI

Small, single-purpose agents, with people in charge

Two kinds of agentsSome help the team do the work. Others are the product RMs use. Each is labeled.
Assistive firstAI drafts or suggests; a named person decides. Autonomy grows only with evidence.
One job eachA clear job, a short list of allowed actions, read-only by default, and a log of everything it does.
Built where the work happensSalesforce for client records, Teams and Outlook for daily work, AWS Bedrock and Snowflake for heavier AI and data.
Choosing the platform
  1. Where RMs spend most of their day
  2. What data the product needs, and where it lives
  3. Fit with the bank's existing approvals and controls
  4. Cost model (per user vs. per use)
  5. Speed to a working pilot
Starting point, to confirm in Discover: if RMs work mainly in Salesforce, show insights on the client record (Agentforce, Prompt Builder) with Snowflake as the data and signal engine, plus a weekly Teams or Outlook digest (Power Automate). Use one AI path; every extra platform adds build work and another review.

3Better data makes better insights

The loop compounds over time, and it's why both goals are built together.

7 · RISKS

Top risks and how I'd handle them

CRM data is incomplete (RMs skip logging)Lean on account data in Snowflake first; the time-saving features improve CRM data over time (the flywheel).
RMs don't trust the insightsShow the reason and numbers behind every insight; co-design with pilot RMs; managers model use.
Governance takes longer than plannedBrief risk partners in week one; keep each wave low-risk, internal-only and on approved platforms; build the evidence pack as we go; if it still runs long, split wave 1 (see the backup plan).
Incentives don't reward acting on insightsAgree recognition for AI-sourced wins with sales leadership before launch.
Costs grow with usageTrack cost per active RM; use the smallest AI model that meets the quality bar.
Revenue attribution is disputedAgree the comparison-group method and tagging with Finance in Define.
Data access takes longer than plannedRequest access in week 1; start with data that's already certified; keep each wave's data needs small.
Client privacy in recorded meetingsClient consent and the bank's recording policy apply; notes stay drafts until the RM approves.
