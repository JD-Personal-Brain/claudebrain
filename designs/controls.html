Controls Catalog

All 25 governance controls in one place: the safeguards built into every AI product, with where each is set, built, proved and watched.

⚠️ Gathered from my own framework and playbook, not from Associated Bank. The bank's own control list comes from its risk partner early in the Define step, and it replaces this one where they differ. Numbers such as 2.4 are sub-steps of my six-step process: look them up in the Steps Catalog. Each control says where it comes from: law or regulator (ask the partner who owns it: compliance, privacy, model risk or vendor risk), bank policy (ask the policy's owner) or our choice (ask the product manager). The automation check never deletes law or bank policy, nor a sub-step that sets, builds, proves or watches them.
