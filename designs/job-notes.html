Part 1: Finding problems

Step 1: Who to talk to first

  • Relationship managers: the bankers who own business clients and bring in new deals.
  • Their sales managers: they see where deals stall across a team.
  • Support staff such as credit analysts and treasury specialists: they do much of the slow behind-the-scenes work.
  • ⚠️ These are common commercial-banking roles. I haven't checked how Associated Bank names or organizes its teams.
  • Open question: Have I worked with bankers or salespeople before?
