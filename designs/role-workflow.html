Inside each step

What happens, and what it takes to pass

Step 4 is the formal approval, not the first time risk partners see the product.

Step 1

Discover

  • Kick off the project (sponsor, who decides, tracker, risk briefing)
  • Frame the outcome with the sponsor
  • Size the opportunity with real data
  • Interview and shadow users
  • Map where their time goes
  • Understand how they're measured and rewarded
  • Test a rough mock-up
  • Pick the first target and record today's numbers
Gate: sponsor agrees on the target, the measure and today's baseline.
Step 2

Define

  • Write the problem and the outcome (one sentence each, measurable)
  • Choose the platform and delivery path
  • Define the data contract (exact fields, freshness, quality checks)
  • Start the risk intake and list the control requirements
  • Write user stories and acceptance criteria
  • Set priorities and record the trade-offs
  • Set the measures (the five kinds, in the Product Framework), quality bar and value model
  • Scope the first release and the pilot group
Gate: brief, data contract and risk rating agreed; value model shows payback within [X] months.
Step 3

Build

  • Design the solution and choose the AI capabilities (agents, retrieval with sources, data flows, workflow automations or system integrations)
  • Prepare trusted data
  • Build the logic: simple rules first, AI explains (it doesn't invent)
  • Build in the controls
  • Deliver it where users work, with the source behind every answer and one-click feedback
  • Build the test set (real examples with known good answers)
  • Test against the quality bar
Gate: meets the quality bar in testing; every control has proof.
Step 4 · Formal approval

Govern and test

  • Confirm the risk rating and register the product
  • Review data and privacy
  • Gather the testing evidence (accuracy, made-up answers, red-teaming)
  • Check fairness
  • Confirm the controls work (human approval, guardrails, audit logs)
  • Confirm the vendor reviews
  • Assemble the evidence pack and get sign-offs from risk, compliance, privacy and security
  • Agree the monitoring plan
Gate: all sign-offs recorded; monitoring plan agreed.
Step 5

Release and adopt

  • Confirm release readiness
  • Launch the pilot, with a comparison group
  • Build it into routines (the core of adoption)
  • Train users on their own work
  • Recruit champions and celebrate wins
  • Fix fast
  • Scale in user waves after a go/no-go check
Gate: weekly use and results hit targets; no serious incidents; long-term owner confirmed before scaling.
Step 6

Measure and iterate

  • Track the measures on a live dashboard
  • Compare with the baseline and the comparison group
  • Re-test after every change, and watch for quality slipping ("drift")
  • Hold the monthly keep / change / stop review
  • Feed the next feature wave
  • Improve the machine itself
Gate: each month (and at the end of each feature wave): keep, change or stop each AI product; each quarter: invest more, adjust or stop overall.
