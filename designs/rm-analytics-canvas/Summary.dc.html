AI-powered RM analytics: the plan on one page

Give relationship managers (RMs) AI insights that help them win new revenue, and free up the time they need to act on them. A product that pays for itself and then some.

THE APPROACH
Deploy a standard base fast AI products most banks need, on platforms the bank already runs, like Salesforce and M365 Copilot, under seven core use cases.
Pair insights with time RMs at many commercial banks spend only about 25–30% of their time with clients, according to McKinsey, so time savers ship alongside insights.
Govern from day one A risk partner from day one. The AI suggests and drafts; the RM decides. Results measured against RMs not yet using it.
THE PLAN (ESTIMATE)
Week 1A working prototype on sample data; RMs react to it in discovery interviews
Weeks 1–2Shape it with RMs; agree data contract, risk rating and value model
Weeks 2–4Meeting follow-up live with pilot RMs
Weeks 4–6Client brief, opportunity list and call notes live, once data and sign-off land
About week 10Wave 2: early warnings, referral prompts
About week 15Wave 3: an RM assistant, prospect lookalikes
About month 6First read on closed revenue: invest more, adjust or stop
What lets us move this fast
  • Build from day one, on sample data
  • Standard features only
  • Low risk by design: internal and assistive
  • Risk partner reviews each version as it's built
  • Data access requested on day one
  • Fast sponsor decisions at each gate
Backup plan The pace is set by data access and AI review, not build time. If either runs long, the meeting follow-up goes first, and the insights and call notes follow by about week 10. The call is made in week 2.
HOW WE'LL KNOW IT WORKS Usage, quality, business impact (pipeline and revenue against a comparison group), adoption, and risk and controls, plus cost per active RM.
WHAT I'D NEED A business sponsor, time with 6–10 RMs and their managers, named data owners, an early risk partner and a willing pilot group.
All timings are estimates. The full plan, with agent specs and workflows, is a separate document.
