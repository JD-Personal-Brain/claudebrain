EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The approach in one page

CORE ASSUMPTION Much of what RMs need is standard across banks: client briefs, meeting follow-ups, product-gap opportunities, early warnings and referral prompts. These can be deployed quickly on platforms the bank already uses. They form the base; everything else is iterated and grown on top.

The plan (estimate) A working prototype in week 1, built alongside discovery. The meeting follow-up goes to pilot RMs in about weeks 2–4, and call notes into Salesforce and the insight features in about weeks 4–6, then small releases every week or two and a new feature wave about every five weeks. The pace is set by data access and AI review, not by build time. A first read on closed revenue comes at about month 6.
