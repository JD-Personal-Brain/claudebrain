# AI-powered RM analytics: one-page summary

> **Retired working copy (2026-10-17).** Out of date: the RM plan is now built around The RM Assistant (memo v10), and the RM Leader Brief replaces this summary (closed item C43). Kept only as a record; don't use it.

_Principal Applied AI Product Manager · Associated Bank · October 2026 ·
the full project plan is a separate document_

**The outcome.** Give relationship managers (RMs) AI insights that help them
win new revenue, and free up the time they need to act on them. The goal is
a product that pays for itself and then some.

**The approach.**
1. **Deploy a standard base fast, aimed at revenue.** Start with AI products most banks need: client briefs before meetings, meeting follow-ups, call notes into Salesforce and a weekly list of product-gap opportunities. They run on platforms the bank already uses: Salesforce, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Power Platform and Snowflake. Each sits under one of seven core use cases (McKinsey's five frontline use cases plus admin off the RM's plate and keep clients), each with a return profile.
2. **Pair insights with time.** RMs at many commercial banks spend only about 25–30% of their time with clients, according to McKinsey, so time savers ship alongside revenue insights.
3. **Govern from day one.** A risk partner is involved from day one. The AI only suggests and drafts; the RM always decides. Results are measured against RMs not yet using the tool.

**The plan (estimate).**

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | A working prototype on sample data; RMs react to it in discovery interviews |
| Weeks 1–2 | Shape it with RMs; agree data contract, risk rating and value model |
| **Weeks 2–4** | **Meeting follow-up live with pilot RMs** |
| **Weeks 4–6** | **Brief, opportunity list and call notes live, once data access and sign-off land** |
| About week 10 | Wave 2: early warnings, referral prompts, prospect lookalikes |
| About week 15 | Wave 3: an RM assistant, AI sales coaching |
| About month 6 | First read on closed revenue: invest more, adjust or stop |

**What lets us move this fast:** build from day one on sample data, standard
features only, low risk by design, a risk partner reviewing each version,
data access requested on day one, and fast gate decisions, in writing when every criterion is met. **Backup:** the
pace is set by data access and AI review, not build time. If either runs long,
the meeting follow-up goes first, and the insights and call notes follow by about week 10.

**How we'll know it works:** usage, quality, business impact (pipeline and
revenue against a comparison group), adoption, and risk and controls, plus
cost per active RM.

**What I'd need:** a business sponsor, time with 6–10 RMs and their
managers, named data owners, an early risk partner and a willing pilot
group.
