# AI-powered RM analytics: one-page summary

_Principal Applied AI Product Manager · Associated Bank · October 2026 ·
the full project plan is a separate document_

**The outcome.** Give relationship managers (RMs) AI insights that help them
win new revenue, and free up the time they need to act on them. The goal is
a product that pays for itself and then some.

**The approach.**
1. **Deploy a standard base fast, aimed at revenue.** Start with use cases most banks need: client briefs before meetings, meeting follow-ups and a weekly list of product-gap opportunities. They run on platforms the bank already uses: Salesforce, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Power Platform and Snowflake.
2. **Pair insights with time.** RMs at many commercial banks reportedly spend only about 25–30% of their time with clients, so time savers ship alongside revenue insights.
3. **Govern from day one.** A risk partner is involved from week 1. The AI only suggests and drafts; the RM always decides. Results are measured against RMs not yet using the tool.

**The plan (estimate).**

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Discover: interview RMs, size the revenue levers, pick wave 1 |
| Weeks 3–4 | Define: brief, data contract, risk rating, value model |
| Weeks 4–7 | Build and test, with formal review in weeks 6–7 |
| **Week 8** | **Wave 1 live with pilot RMs:** client brief, meeting follow-up, weekly opportunity list |
| About week 15 | Wave 2: early warnings, call notes into Salesforce, referral prompts |
| About week 21 | Wave 3: an RM assistant, prospect lookalikes |
| About month 6 | First read on closed revenue; invest more, adjust or stop |

**What makes 8 weeks possible:** standard features only, low risk by design,
evidence built during the build, data access requested in week 1, and fast
sponsor decisions. **Backup:** if review or data access runs long, the
meeting follow-up still goes live in about week 8 and the insights follow
by about week 14.

**How we'll know it works:** usage, quality, business impact (pipeline and
revenue against a comparison group), adoption, and risk and controls, plus
cost per active RM.

**What I'd need:** a business sponsor, time with 6–10 RMs and their
managers, named data owners, an early risk partner and a willing pilot
group.
