    The five kinds of measures · Product Framework

    Product manager view

    How we'd know whether RMs use it, trust it and win more with it. These counts come from this session; in a pilot they come from the live dashboard (sub-step 6.1), against a comparison group.

    How we'd get RMs to use it

    • Built into a routine managers already run: the Monday list could go into the weekly pipeline meeting (our assumption, to confirm with the bank; sub-step 5.3).
    • Trained on their own work: each RM's first session uses their own book, not a demo (5.4).
    • One click of feedback on every output steers what gets better next (5.6).
    • Champions show peers how they use it, and wins get celebrated (5.5).
    • This tour is the first-time guide: it plays once, and anyone can replay it.

    The data

    • Sample book: 12 made-up business clients for one RM, plus 2 belonging to another RM (used only to show the refusal).
    • Why a saved copy: a published page can't reach outside websites, so the build script fetches the data and bakes it in with its date. In production, our assumption (to confirm with the bank) is that the agents read certified data from the bank's own systems.
