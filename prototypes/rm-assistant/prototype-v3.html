      The five kinds of measures

      How we'd measure it

      How we'd know whether RMs use it, trust it and win more with it. The big numbers count what happened in this session, the tour included. Under each, a made-up sample pilot week (8 pilot RMs) shows what a real week might look like; in a pilot the numbers come from a live dashboard, compared with a group of RMs who don't have the assistant yet.

      How we'd get RMs to use it

      • Built into a routine managers already run: the Monday list could go into the weekly pipeline meeting (our assumption, to confirm with the bank).
      • Trained on their own work: each RM's first session uses their own clients, not a demo.
      • Use, change or reject on every output: each click steers what gets better next.
      • Champions show peers how they use it, and wins get celebrated.
      • This tour is the first-time guide: it plays once, and anyone can replay it.

      The data

      • Sample clients: 12 made-up business clients for one RM, plus 2 belonging to another RM, used only to show the refusal.
      • Why a saved copy: a published page can't reach outside websites, so the build fetches the data and saves it with its date. The outside feed is a prototype addition; a real build would add it to the data contract (K5). In production, our assumption (to confirm with the bank) is that the agents read certified data from the bank's own systems.

      How it's tested

      Automated tests click through every promise on this page, with a stand-in for Claude: the tour, each agent, the outside data, the guardrails (risky phrasings blocked and innocent questions allowed), approval before saving, the number check with planted made-up numbers, the stale-data stop, laptop and phone layouts, and the recorded-run fallback. In a real build this grows into the test set and quality bar (K9).

      What a pilot would need (our assumption, to confirm)

      • Data: read access to certified client and CRM data for the pilot RMs' clients. This usually sets the pace, not the build.
      • People: a sponsor, a risk partner from day one, a data owner, and a small pilot group of RMs with a comparison group.
      • Next: interviews and shadowing with RMs, then the first capability in front of them within weeks. The plan is on the RM Assistant Plan page.
